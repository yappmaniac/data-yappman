Monthly tables

Median sold prices by postcode district: new table for 2026-10. 2,526 districts, of which 2,255 have a 12-month median for all homes; the median of those medians is £292,500. The sales are standard sales in England and Wales by transfer date, from 2025-08 to 2026-07, ending 3 months before the table month to allow for registration. The newest months are revised as late registrations arrive.

Local authority codes: Source: Office for National Statistics licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database right 2026. Contains Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right 2026.

Gross rental yield by local authority: new table for 2026-10. 316 authorities, of which 316 have an all-homes yield; the median of those yields is 4.3%. It is gross, before costs, voids, tax and finance, and it is not advice. The rent is a mean and the price a median, and the homes rented and the homes sold differ, so these are area averages, not a valuation. The rents come from the ONS Price Index of Private Rents (PIPR). PIPR figures are statistics in development and are not seasonally adjusted.
