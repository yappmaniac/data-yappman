Free sample: UK gender pay gap 2025-26

A free 2,000-row sample of the UK gender pay gap reporting data for the 2025-26 reporting year, employers with a Companies House number only. Retrieved 19 November 2026.

Download the CSV (2,000 rows, about 1 MB) Data dictionary (Markdown)

SHA-256 of the CSV: 3e2f7c368db589a4cee8012f285022d9ba8b1494b536db36d6d45ee8b5c070b0.

Source and selection

  • Source: gender-pay-gap.service.gov.uk, 2025 download, retrieved 19 November 2026.
  • Selection: a random sample (seed 19) of 2,000 of the 11,202 rows that have a company number. It is a sample of the public file, not the full register.
