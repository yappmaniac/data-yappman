UK Online Shelf-Price Index
https://data.yappman.com/shelf-price-index/

Download: uk-shelf-price-index.csv and uk-shelf-price-index.json
The data is in long format: one row per vintage, record type, retailer and class. The JSON holds the same figures as the dataset, the meta item first in each vintage. The CSV holds the rows without the meta items, with one more column at the end, base: the base week of that vintage's series (2026-W39 for weeks 39 and 40, 2026-W42 from week 42).
Fields, record types, statuses and meta: https://data.yappman.com/shelf-price-index/data-dictionary/
Shelf prices only; it is not CPI and not a measure of prices paid.
