Gross rental yield by local authority, England and Wales

Updated monthly. The gross yield is ONS’s average monthly rent (on new and existing tenancies) times 12, against the HM Land Registry median price of standard sales (category A), for the same property type, the same local authority and the same 12 months.

It is gross, before costs, voids, tax and finance, and it is not advice. The rent is a mean and the price a median, and the homes rented and the homes sold differ, so these are area averages, not a valuation.

The rents come from the ONS Price Index of Private Rents (PIPR). PIPR figures are statistics in development and are not seasonally adjusted.

Scotland and Northern Ireland are not covered: PIPR publishes them by Broad Rental Market Area, and HM Land Registry has no data for them. City of London and the Isles of Scilly are not covered either: PIPR does not publish them.

On a property-type row the yield is withheld, shown as –, when the type has fewer than 20 sales, or when it is below half of the authority’s all-homes yield, which we take as a sign that the rented and the sold homes of that type are not alike. The rent, sales and median are still given.

For outcode and bedroom level from current listings, use the UK Property Listings Actor’s rentalYield summary.

Method

Every row

A – without a note means there is no figure: fewer than 5 sales, or a rent PIPR does not publish for all 12 months.
