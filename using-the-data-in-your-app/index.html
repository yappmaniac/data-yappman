Using the data in your app

For people building a meal-planning, nutrition, budgeting or shopping-list app with UK supermarket prices in it.

Some links to Apify are affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission.

This page says what we supply, what we ask of you and what we cannot give you. It is a guide: general information, not legal advice, and not a licence.

Two ways to get UK prices

1. The free daily basket. A fixed list of 212 staple products at Tesco, Sainsbury’s, Asda and Aldi, read daily. It is a public dataset under CC BY 4.0, and a monthly snapshot is on Kaggle. You need no account. We chose the 212 products, so your own list may not be in it.

2. The UK grocery Actor on the Apify Store. You run UK Supermarket & Grocery Prices on your own Apify account, for the products you choose, when you choose. Give it the product page links for your list, save that as a Task, and put the Task on a weekly schedule. Each run returns one row per product with the price, the pack size and the time it was read. The rows from a run belong to the account that ran it, under Apify’s terms.

What we ask, and what we do not limit

For rows from your own runs of the Actor:

  • We place no limit of our own on showing or keeping the prices a run returns. We cannot give you rights over the retailers’ content or data; their terms may apply to your use.
  • Please show the date each price was read (the scrapedAt field), or make clear that prices are a snapshot. Shop prices change.
  • We ask you not to resell the rows or pass them on in bulk; if you need that, contact us. Passing them on in bulk includes offering them as a file, a feed or an API of your own.

For the free daily basket, the condition is the CC BY 4.0 licence: give credit, link the licence, and say if you changed the data. We set no other condition on it.

What we cannot give you

  • We have no permission from any retailer and claim none. We cannot grant any right on a retailer’s behalf.
  • The retailers’ website terms restrict automated or commercial use and claim rights in their site content and data. Those terms may apply to what you do with the prices. Checking that is for you; we give no view on it.
  • Our CC BY 4.0 licence applies to our compilation, so far as we hold rights in it. We claim no rights in the prices, product names or identifiers themselves.
  • Product images, descriptions and ingredient text are the retailers’ content. They are not ours and we do not license them.
  • The data is provided as it was collected. We give no warranty of rights in it and no indemnity.

Credit

For the free daily basket: “Source: Yappman Data, UK Supermarket Prices: Daily 212-Item Basket, CC BY 4.0”, with a link to the licence.

For rows from your own runs of the Actor we do not require a credit. If you want to give one, “Prices read by Yappman Data on [date]” is accurate. Please do not write anything that suggests a retailer supplies or approves the prices, or that we are connected with a retailer.

What it costs

The free basket costs nothing. The Actor is charged by Apify per run and per product returned: on Apify’s Free plan, $0.005 a run plus $0.0025 a product, and less per product on higher Apify plans. The Actor’s Store page gives the current prices and is the place to check them. A product that returns no row is not charged. Apify’s own plan fees and free allowance are Apify’s; see their pricing page.

If you need longer history

  • Keep your own. Add outputDataset to your input and each weekly run adds its rows to one named dataset in your account.
  • Ask the Actor. Switch on includePriceHistory and each row carries the weekly prices we stored for that product in earlier weeks, starting from week 42 of 2026 (the collection of 18 October 2026). It is charged per row; see the Store page.
  • For whole weekly files, see our licences page. Those are supplied under a written licence.

How to stop

You. Switch off the schedule or delete the Task in your Apify account. There is no contract with us, no minimum term and nothing to cancel with us.

Us. We do not promise that any shop will stay available. If a shop refuses a request, that run returns no rows for that shop. If a shop asks us in writing to stop, we stop reading it ourselves and remove it from the Actor in its next version. Build your app so that a missing price or a missing shop does not break it.

If you are a retailer or a rights holder and want us to stop or to correct something, write to info@yappman.com.

Not covered here

This page is about UK prices. We do not have Lidl or M&S. Nutrition and ingredient data is not something we license. For Australia this site publishes the shelf-price index only; this page does not cover Australian prices.
