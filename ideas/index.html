Tools we are considering

We are gauging interest. No payment is taken and no price is set.

The five pages

  • Supermarket price alerts: would you use them?. An email, sent for you, telling you when the shelf price of a product you choose changes at a UK supermarket, for example a fall below a price you set.
  • A split-basket optimiser: would you use it?. A tool that takes your shopping list and says which items to buy at which UK supermarket so the list might cost less than at one shop.
  • A shrinkflation tracker: would you use it?. A list of UK supermarket products whose pack size apparently became smaller while the shelf price stayed the same or rose, week by week.
  • A brand price and promotion tracker: would you use it?. A weekly report for a brand or a list of products: shelf prices, was-prices, promotions and loyalty prices at each UK supermarket, and what changed since the week before.
  • Food hygiene rating alerts: would you use them?. An email telling you when the food hygiene rating of a business you follow changes.

The way to answer is the email address and subject each page gives. Each page says what to include.
