    Open data

    • Public contractsLatestSearch UK public procurement notices. $0.005 a notice ($5.00 per 1,000) plus $0.005 a run.
    • Australian contract notices (AusTender)On the Apify StoreLook up, search and monitor Australian Government contract awards, read from the AusTender API.
    • LEI lookupLatestLegal Entity Identifier records by LEI, name or registration status. $0.002 an entity ($2.00 per 1,000), no start fee.
    • CRICOS register lookup (Australia)On the Apify StoreAustralian education providers registered to enrol overseas students: one row each with type, state, postcode, city, capacity and course counts. $0.002 a row ($2.00 per 1,000), no start fee. Not affiliated with the Department of Education.
    • UK importers and exportersOn the Apify StoreHMRC trade lists by month, under the Open Government Licence. $0.005 a run plus $0.002 a trader row. Not affiliated with HMRC.
    • UK sponsor register changesOn the Apify StoreDated changes to the UK register of licensed sponsors, one row per change. $0.003 a change row ($3.00 per 1,000), no start fee. Not affiliated with the Home Office or UK Visas and Immigration.
    • CQC England care locationsOn the Apify StoreSearch and look up care locations in the CQC directory for England, and list what changed between its files. $0.005 a run plus $0.004 a location or $0.003 a change. Not affiliated with the Care Quality Commission.
    • Approved food establishments (FSA)On the Apify StoreApproved food establishments in England, Wales and Northern Ireland from the Food Standards Agency daily file: lookup, search and changes. Scotland is not covered. Not affiliated with the Food Standards Agency.
    • GB gambling business registerOn the Apify StoreLook up and watch the Gambling Commission register of gambling businesses for Great Britain, with dated changes. Not affiliated with the Gambling Commission.
    • UK and EU product recallsOn the Apify StoreSearch and monitor product recalls and safety alerts from UK OPSS, FSA and MHRA and from EU Safety Gate, in one row schema.
    • EU air and sea traffic (Eurostat)On the Apify StoreEurostat airport passengers, flights and freight, route pairs and port traffic for EU and EFTA countries.
