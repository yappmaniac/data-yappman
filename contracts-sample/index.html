Free sample: UK public procurement notices (Find a Tender and Contracts Finder)

A free sample of 208 tender, award and contract notices published on 27 October 2026 on the two UK government services.

Some links to Apify are affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission.

Download the CSV (208 rows, about 70 KB)

SHA-256 of the CSV: 46e0b6ad57af85f5518f7d30aaea6519c91ddade69992be53d118ab3a7c690bb.

What the sample is

  • 208 notices published on 27 October 2026 on the Find a Tender Service and Contracts Finder, all stages (planning, tender, award, contract, termination).
  • Only the buyer organisation and the notice title are free text. Descriptions, contact points, addresses, supplier names and party identifiers are not included; email addresses, UK phone numbers and names written after phrases like “contact” or “FAO” in titles are replaced by pattern, which does not catch every written name.
  • A sample, not the full set: notices are amended after publication, and the noticeUrl column points to each notice’s public page.

Columns

ColumnMeaning
ColumnDescription
sourceService the notice came from: fts (Find a Tender) or cf (Contracts Finder).
noticeIdFind a Tender notice id or Contracts Finder notice GUID, as published.
ocidContracting process id (OCID), as published on Find a Tender / Contracts Finder; empty if none.
relatedOcidsOCIDs of the same procurement on another service, only where the notice references them; empty in this sample.
reconcileKeyHash of normalised buyer and title; matching keys across services suggest one procurement, not a guarantee.
releaseIdId of the notice version returned, as published on Find a Tender / Contracts Finder.
titleNotice title, as published on Find a Tender / Contracts Finder, with personal details redacted.
buyerBuyer organisation name, as published on Find a Tender / Contracts Finder.
valueEstimated value, or the sum of awarded amounts on award notices, as published; empty if not given.
currencyISO 4217 currency code of the value column.
cpvCodesCPV codes on the notice, 8 digits each, separated by semicolons.
regionsUK ITL1 regions of delivery, or Outside UK, separated by semicolons.
publishedAtPublication time of the notice version (ISO 8601, UTC), as published.
deadlineTender deadline (ISO 8601, UTC), as published; empty if none.
stageNotice stage: planning, tender, award, contract or termination.
noticeUrlLink to the public notice page on Find a Tender or Contracts Finder.
