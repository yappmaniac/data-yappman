UK Christmas dinner basket 2026

Posted online shelf prices for a fixed Christmas dinner basket at Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda and Aldi, read from pinned product pages. Intended daily; gaps are listed in the notes.

It is not CPI, and it is not a measure of prices paid. These are posted online shelf prices read from product pages, with no quantities and no transactions. Treat them as an indicator of what four retailers are asking online for one fixed basket.

Method

  • 27 items, one pinned product each, at four retailers. Each pinned product page is intended to be read once a day. Item numbers are kept from the original 28-item list, so there is no item 27.
  • Own-label standard tier. Premium, value, organic and free-from lines are not used, except the chocolate selection box, where a branded box is normal.
  • One pack size per item. Where a retailer has no product at that size the nearest size is used and the cell is flagged “size differs”. Prices and totals use the shelf price of the pinned pack, so items sold by weight, such as the turkey and the joints, are not scaled to a common weight. Turkeys carry a fresh or frozen flag.
  • Optional items. Beef roasting joint, gammon joint and orange juice are shown but left out of every total.
  • Gaps. listed when the summary loads.
  • Prices posted online. Online catalogues only. No store is chosen for Tesco and Sainsbury's, and one fixed store or service point is used for Asda and Aldi, so prices may differ in other stores, formats, regions and channels.
