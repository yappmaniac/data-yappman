How much history we hold

The weeks of shelf-price snapshots we hold, by retailer, and what exists before them. Weekly, not real time. These are posted online shelf prices, not prices paid.

Some links to Apify are affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission.

The short answer

Our weekly UK snapshots start with the collection of 27 September 2026 (ISO week 2026-W39). The latest weekly release is 2026-W45, published 9 November 2026. The Australian index starts with 2026-W41. The history is short: one week is added with each weekly release, and the tables below are rebuilt from the published index files, so this page cannot say more than the index does.

Why there is nothing earlier

We began collecting the weekly UK snapshots on 27 September 2026 and the daily basket on 24 September 2026 (it has no run on 25 September). Nothing was collected before those dates, so there are no earlier weeks to supply, and we do not estimate or backfill them. Morrisons is held from week 44 of 2026.

The daily basket

The daily 212-item basket is a public dataset under CC BY 4.0, with history from 24 September 2026; a monthly snapshot of it is also on Kaggle.

Product-level weeks

With the includePriceHistory option, the search, category and product rows of our UK Supermarket & Grocery Prices Actor on the Apify Store carry the product’s weekly snapshot prices, newest first, up to 52 weeks, from ISO week 39 of 2026 (week 41 is missing at Tesco, Sainsbury’s and Asda; there are none for Morrisons). The Actor builds no weekly report. Price history is not offered for Morrisons yet, so its rows carry an empty price history and are never charged for it; the Morrisons weekly snapshots we hold (from 2026-W44) are not yet part of that.

Retailer names are used only to describe the source of public product information; no affiliation, endorsement or partnership is claimed. Shelf prices only; not CPI and not a measure of prices paid. How the snapshots are checked is on the data quality page.
