City Hotel Prices

A matched-hotel change index for city hotel prices. Base 100 at ISO week 2026-W40. Published as static aggregate files under CC BY 4.0. The first release follows the week-42 run on Sunday 18 October 2026 and is planned for Monday 19 October, after review; it carries two weekly changes (weeks 41 and 42).

What it measures

The index follows the change in the prices of the same (matched) hotels from one week to the next. It is a change index: each week is compared with the week before and chained from a base of 100 at ISO week 2026-W40.
