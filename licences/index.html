Data licences

Annual licences for the UK supermarket price files, with a one-month paid pilot in the same files as the paid product.

What you can license

What a row holds, and how the files are checked, is set out in the methodology and on the data quality page. Licensed files start at week 42 of 2026 (the collection of 18 October 2026) and grow by one week each week. Weeks 39 and 40 were collected at the four shops; in week 41 only the Aldi collection completed (see the notice). None of weeks 39 to 41 is supplied: the run records for those weeks no longer exist, so we cannot show whether a proxy was used, and we supply raw files only for retailers read direct. Morrisons is not part of the licensed files for now. The weeks we hold, by retailer, are on the coverage page. Published index values are not revised except to correct an error.

We have no permission from any retailer and claim none. Prices are facts. Retailer content (images, descriptions, ingredient text, logos) is not ours and is not licensed by us. We give no warranty of rights.

Hotel and property data: on request.

Paid pilot

A one-month paid pilot is offered in the same files as the paid product. For the weekly catalogue the pilot month costs £600 plus VAT, and the fee is credited against the annual licence fee if you take an annual licence for the same series within 30 days of the pilot ending.

Without a licence

  • The weekly UK and Australian shelf-price indices are free under CC BY 4.0.
