Data licences

Annual licences for the UK supermarket price files, with a one-month paid pilot in the same files as the paid product.

What you can license

Annual licence per series, per buyer entity. The price below is indicative. All prices exclude VAT.

SeriesCoverageFrequencyRows per periodHistory fromLicence
UK weekly catalogueTesco, Sainsbury’s, Asda, Aldi, Morrisons (Morrisons from week 44)Weekly (collected Sunday)Over 75,000 (four retailers); Morrisons is additional27 Sep 2026 (Morrisons: week 44)from £6,000 a year

What a row holds, and how the files are checked, is set out in the methodology and on the data quality page. History accumulates from the dates shown. The weekly catalogue has no complete data for Tesco, Sainsbury’s and Asda for the week of 11 October 2026 (see the notice). Morrisons is held from week 44 of 2026 (2026-W44). Published index values are not revised except to correct an error.

Retailer content (images, descriptions, ingredient text, logos) is not ours and not licensed by us.

Hotel and property data: on request.

Paid pilot

A one-month paid pilot is offered in the same files as the paid product. For the weekly catalogue the pilot month costs £600 plus VAT, and the fee is credited against the annual licence fee if you take an annual licence for the same series within 30 days of the pilot ending.
