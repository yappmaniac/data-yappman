Data quality

How complete and accurate our data is, one section per Actor, with known gaps stated plainly. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

How we measure

For UK grocery, barcode coverage, completeness and match accuracy are counted from the latest weekly snapshot (one row per product per retailer; week and date stated with each figure); run success is read from the canary runs' metadata. For the other Actors, barcode coverage is the share of rows in the sample stated with each figure that carry a barcode (EAN/GTIN), and completeness is the share of those rows that have a value for the field. Run success rate is the share of scheduled canary runs that succeeded over the stated number of days; a canary is a small smoke test, so it shows that a run can reach its sources, not that every field is complete. Where we checked matches by hand, the sample size and method are stated with the figure. Every figure comes from the snapshot files, run metadata, canaries or hand checks and is dated. None is estimated, and where we have no measurement the row is left out.

The weekly indices built from the grocery snapshots have their own method pages: UK and AU. How the data is collected is in the methodology.

Freshness is not measured: run success is the platform status, and each run reads the source's pages at its start time (scrapedAt); a source serving a stale page to everyone would not be detected.

Known gaps

  • Aldi publishes no barcodes and no stock status, and its products rarely share an exact name with another retailer's, so most Aldi rows can't be joined to other retailers automatically; compare them by unit price.

Known gaps

  • Aldi publishes no stock status (inStock is null).
  • Coles answers about 80 requests per run, so a run returns at most a few thousand Coles products (about 75 with barcode lookup on), not the whole range; the weekly Coles snapshot behind the AU index reads staples first.
  • Woolworths prices are for the store Woolworths assigns to a logged-out session: usually 1101, sometimes a regional store, which changes the range (4 of 21 scheduled canary runs from 26 September to 16 October 2026, all before AU 0.1.13, which retries for the national store). Every row records its store in storeId; the weekly snapshot and the AU index use store 1101 only.
  • Special Buys are returned by their own mode; the weekly Aldi snapshot behind the AU index covers the regular range only.
