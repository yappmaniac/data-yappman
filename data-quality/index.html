How we measure

Barcode coverage is the share of rows in the latest full run that carry a barcode (EAN/GTIN). Match accuracy is the share of a hand-checked sample of cross-retailer matches that were correct; the sample size and how it was drawn are stated with each figure. Run success rate is the share of scheduled canary runs that succeeded over the stated number of days. Completeness is the share of rows in the latest full run that have a value for the field. Every figure is read from the Actor's own run metadata and canaries, not estimated, and is dated above.
