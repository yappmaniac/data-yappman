How we measure

Barcode coverage is the share of rows in the sample stated with each figure that carry a barcode (EAN/GTIN), and completeness is the share of those rows that have a value for the field. Run success rate is the share of scheduled canary runs that succeeded over the stated number of days; a canary is a small smoke test, so it shows that a run can reach its sources, not that every field is complete. Where we checked matches by hand, the sample size and method are stated with the figure. Every figure comes from the Actor's own run metadata, canaries or hand checks and is dated; none is estimated, and where we have no measurement the row is left out.

Known gaps

  • Aldi publishes no stock status (inStock is null).
  • Coles answers about 80 requests per run, so a run returns at most a few thousand Coles products (about 75 with barcode lookup on), not the whole range; the weekly Coles snapshot behind the AU index reads staples first.
  • Woolworths prices are for the store Woolworths assigns to a logged-out session: usually 1101, sometimes a regional store, which changes the range (2 of 8 scheduled canary runs on 5–12 October 2026). Every row records its store in storeId; the weekly snapshot and the AU index use store 1101 only.
  • Special Buys are returned by their own mode; the weekly Aldi snapshot behind the AU index covers the regular range only.
