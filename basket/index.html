How this works


What this is. A like-for-like check of 98 everyday items that we chose, at four shops. It is not a survey of every product and it does not show which shop is lowest overall.


How items were chosen. We wrote the list by hand: common staples at common sizes. Premium, organic, free-from and flavoured lines are left out. Products were never chosen by price. Each item is either the same branded product at each shop (“same product”) or each shop’s own standard product of the same kind and size (“comparable own-label”). Comparable own-label is our judgement about the kind of product, not its quality or recipe. A person checked every match on 17 November 2026. The full list, with each product’s name and pack size at each shop, is above.


Prices. Each price is for one item without a loyalty card, as read without signing in. A temporary price cut is already included. Multi-buy offers, vouchers and personal offers are never applied. Loyalty-card prices are left out unless you switch them on on this page. An Aldi price may be a cut that is not marked. Where an offer is listed for an item, the cell says so. Totals are for goods only: no delivery, collection or bag charges and no minimum spend. Unit prices are worked out by us from the price and the pack size shown.


Where. Tesco and Sainsbury’s: prices are read with no store chosen. Asda and Aldi: prices are read for one fixed store or service area, so they may differ from the price at your local store. Prices in smaller-format stores or for your address can differ. Aldi stock is not shown in our data.



When. Prices are read once a day, early morning UK time. Every price shows the date it was read. Shops change prices during the day. The shop’s own price at the till or checkout is the one that counts.


Sizes. Same-product packs are within 3% in size. Comparable own-label packs are the same stated size; where a size differs the cell says so and is left out of totals unless you choose to include it.


Changes. If a product disappears we show “no matching product” until a person picks a replacement; replacements and corrections are listed with dates below. Corrections so far: none.


Who we are and how this is paid for. We are Yappman Data; the company name, number and registered office are in the footer of every page, and our email address is info@yappman.com. The page is free. No shop pays us or supplies us, and there are no shop affiliate links here. We sell data tools and data licences, described on the licences page.


Not advice, not a government statistic, not a measure of what people paid.



Aldi, Asda, Sainsbury’s and Tesco are named only to say where each price was read. Morrisons is named only to say it is not included. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or supplied by any of them, and we do not have their permission. Clubcard Prices and Nectar Prices are the shops’ own scheme names. Product and brand names belong to their owners.


To correct a price or match, write to info@yappman.com; we normally reply within one working day. If a shop asks us in writing to stop, we stop collecting and showing its prices the day we read the message.
