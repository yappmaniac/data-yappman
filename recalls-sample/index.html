Free sample: UK and EU product recalls and safety alerts

A free 97-row sample of recalls and alerts first published in the 60 days to 19 November 2026 by four public sources. Read on 19 November 2026.

Some links to Apify are affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission.

Download the CSV (97 rows, about 126 KB)

SHA-256 of the CSV: 96138050e78ad226c9cb9c3060707d71708c0ef7290d44c45f59acdaa3ca68aa.

What the sample is

The most recent rows first published in the 60 days to 19 November 2026, from four public sources: 33 UK OPSS product recalls and alerts, 15 UK FSA food alerts, 14 UK MHRA drug and device alerts and 35 EU Safety Gate alerts. The target was 100 rows; 3 rows first published before the window were dropped. It was built from 43 requests, one a second, with robots.txt obeyed, no key and no login.

It is a snapshot for looking at the schema and the sources, not a maintained dataset. Items change and are withdrawn; the url column points to the publisher's page for each item. It is not safety, legal or medical advice.

Privacy

E-mail addresses, phone numbers, images and attachments are removed. Company and brand names are as the publisher printed them; rows can name sole traders.
