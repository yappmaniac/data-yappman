Free sample: EU airport months and port quarters (Eurostat)

Two small CSV files: a dated copy of what Eurostat had published on 19 November 2026, in the columns the Actor returns.

Airport months CSV (12 rows, about 5 KB) Port quarters CSV (32 rows, about 11 KB)

SHA-256 of airport_months_sample.csv: 16b53d3db3a6afb343e110f251013600791a30e2f652133e2728e1dddeb6d0b2.
SHA-256 of port_quarters_sample.csv: 58be73c6434d0d6944358e4796efcaf387be4d7611ade1c8643dd1adafb3af39.

What the sample is

  • airport_months_sample.csv: 12 of the busiest airports among the countries that had values for August 2026 (13 countries; Germany, Spain, France and Italy had none for that month yet), one row per airport, month 2026-08. Source dataset: avia_paoa.
  • port_quarters_sample.csv: Dutch ports, goods (thousand tonnes) for 2025-Q4 and 2026-Q1 and passengers (thousand, cruise passengers excluded) for 2026-Q1 and 2026-Q2. Source datasets: mar_go_qm_nl and mar_pa_qm_nl.

The files are a snapshot, not a feed. Values can be revised by Eurostat, and a blank cell means Eurostat had no value, not zero. They do not cover every airport or port.
