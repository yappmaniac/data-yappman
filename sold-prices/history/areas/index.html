Median sold prices by local authority and region, England and Wales

From 1995, by year and by quarter, from HM Land Registry Price Paid Data.

This page gives the median sold price, and the lower and upper quartile, for each local authority and each region of England and Wales, by calendar year and by calendar quarter from 1995, for one split at a time. Pick a region, then an authority or the whole region, years or quarters, and a split; or download the files. The table is free to use under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

What these figures are, and are not

  • Standard sales only (category A), by transfer date, in nominal pounds. The figures are not adjusted for inflation.
  • The splits are one grouping at a time and are not combined: all sales, one property type, one tenure, or new-build or existing. Quarters have all sales and the four property types only.
  • There is a break in late 2013. Before October 2013 every sale was in the standard category. From October 2013 some sales (repossessions, buy-to-lets where identifiable, transfers to companies, and property type “other”) are recorded in a separate category that this table leaves out. Years from 2014 therefore cover a different share of all sales from earlier years. Compare across 2013 with care.
  • This is not an index. The mix of homes sold changes from period to period.
  • Authorities are today’s authorities applied back to 1995: a sale counts in the authority its postcode is in today, not the one it was in at the time. Sales whose postcode could not be placed in an authority are in no area: 786 sales in this build. They are left out of every authority and region figure.
  • Regions are the nine English regions of the Office for National Statistics lookup. Wales is shown as one area, and its authorities appear under it. England and Wales only; Scotland and Northern Ireland are not covered.
  • Medians and quartiles are left blank, and shown as a dash, where a period has fewer than 5 sales. A period with fewer than 20 sales is marked as a small sample. With exactly 5 sales the lower quartile, median and upper quartile are real sale prices that HM Land Registry already publishes.
  • The latest period is as at the month the file was built. Sales registered later are added when the file is next rebuilt, which happens when a new quarter has ended or the postcode release changes.

Category A (standard price paid) sales only, by transfer date, one row per local authority or region, period (calendar year or calendar quarter) and split (a split is one grouping at a time: all sales, one property type, one tenure, or new-build or existing; they are not combined; quarters have all sales and the four property types only). Medians and quartiles (inclusive method) in whole pounds, nominal and not adjusted for inflation; null when a period has fewer than 5 sales (a quality threshold: every sale is already published by HM Land Registry). Rows with no sales are left out. Only complete periods are shown; the latest period is as at the month the file was built, and sales registered later are added when the file is next rebuilt (when a new quarter has ended or the postcode release changes). Authorities are the current authorities of the ONS Postcode Directory release named in the file, applied back to 1995: a sale counts in the authority its postcode is in today, not the authority it was in at the time. Sales whose postcode could not be placed in an authority are counted in no area. Regions are the English regions of the ONS lookup; Wales is one area. England and Wales only. Not an index: the mix of homes sold changes from period to period.
