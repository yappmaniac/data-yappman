What we provide

Shelf prices, promotions and, where a retailer publishes one, loyalty prices for every product the major supermarkets list online, joined across retailers by EAN barcode where the retailer publishes one. A weekly full-catalogue series for the UK and Australia, and a daily fixed basket of 212 UK staples for consistent day-to-day tracking. Retail-level price data with this structure is a nowcast of food inflation weeks ahead of official statistics, and a competitor-price series for CPG and retail analytics.

Fields Supermarket data

One row per product per retailer per period: retailer, retailer product id, EAN, name, brand, pack size, shelf price, was-price, unit price and measure, loyalty price, promotion text and end date, stock flag, category path, URL, image URL; rating and review count where the retailer shows them; timestamp; and, in the Australian series, currency, market and store id. Loyalty price where the retailer publishes one: Tesco Clubcard, Sainsbury's Nectar and Morrisons More Card. Asda, Aldi, Coles, Woolworths and Aldi Australia publish no loyalty price, so the field is empty for them. Shelf price is always the price a non-member pays; loyalty pricing is never folded into it.

How it is collected Supermarket data

1

Source. The retailers' own public websites, read logged out. No accounts, no baskets, no personal data of any kind.

2

Method. Automated reads of the pages and JSON any visitor receives. Requests are made one at a time, at no more than two a second per retailer (one a second for Morrisons); in practice about 0.6 to 1.5 a second on average. Nothing a retailer's published robots.txt disallows is ever requested; paths were checked against each source's robots.txt where one could be read.

3

Quality. Daily canary runs for every retailer, an automated weekly health review of every canary, snapshot and index build, and email alerts on failed runs. Weekly reconciliation of each retailer's product count against the previous week and the first full week; a drop of more than 10% is flagged. Parsers that fail loudly rather than emit partial rows; missed periods recorded as gaps, never interpolated.

4

Change control. A change log ships with every delivery; schema and coverage changes are announced 30 days ahead, under Notices.

Delivery Supermarket data

Weekly (and, for the basket, daily) CSV files, one per retailer per period, as a download. Parquet, and delivery to your S3 bucket, SFTP or a signed download link, are available on request. The live data is also available programmatically through the Apify API. A basket-pricing endpoint (price a list of barcodes across all retailers in one call) is in preparation. Weekly data is available by 08:00 UTC on Sunday; the daily basket by 07:00 UTC.

Due diligence Supermarket data

A methodology and due-diligence document is available on request. It covers provenance, collection method, robots.txt compliance, the terms-of-service position, database-right and copyright treatment, personal data (none), anti-bot handling, audit fields, coverage limits, accuracy checks, retention and corporate details. Retailer names are used only to describe the source of public product information; no affiliation, endorsement or partnership is claimed.
