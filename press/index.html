Press

For journalists and writers: the UK Online Shelf-Price Index, how to cite it, and who to contact.

The UK Online Shelf-Price Index in brief

The UK Online Shelf-Price Index reads about 73,000 products a week in its weekly snapshots at Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda and Aldi, and matches the same products week to week. It is point-in-time data, intended to be published weekly, normally on a Monday, when the week's snapshots pass the method's checks. Published index values are not revised except to correct an error. It measures posted shelf prices, not prices paid, so it is not CPI. The series started with a base of 100 at ISO week 2026-W39; week 2026-W41 was skipped after an incomplete collection, so the index restarted with a new base at week 42 (see the notice).

Method in brief

  • What it is. An index of posted online shelf prices at Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda and Aldi: products matched week on week, combined with an unweighted geometric mean (a Jevons index) within each retailer and class, equal weight to each retailer, chained from a base of 100.
  • Where the prices are read. No store is chosen for Tesco and Sainsbury's; one fixed store or service point for Asda and Aldi. Prices in other stores, formats, regions and channels may differ.
  • What it is not. Not the consumer prices index (CPI), not a forecast of it, not prices paid and not household cost of living.
  • Kept as first published. Published index values are not revised except to correct an error. Full method.

Data and chart

  • Published series: CSV or JSON (index numbers and counts only).
  • Chart: the headline series chart on the index page.
  • The Australian index has its own page, with its reference stores stated.

Using the figures

The free index and basket datasets listed on this site are released under CC BY 4.0: the UK weekly index dataset, the Australian weekly index dataset and the daily 212-item basket dataset (including its Kaggle copy and same-week copies we publish elsewhere). You may copy, share and adapt them, including commercially, as long as you credit Yappman Data, link to the licence and say if you changed anything.

Not covered by that licence: paid per-retailer catalogues and snapshots, private index chains, the output of anyone's own Actor run, the Analyst pack, and retailer content (images, descriptions, ingredient text, logos), which is not ours and not licensed by us.

Retailer names are used only to describe the source of public product information; no affiliation, endorsement or partnership is claimed.

Latest finding
