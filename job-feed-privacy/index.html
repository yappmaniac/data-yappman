Job feed: how we handle personal details in job adverts

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who we are. Abley-Yapp Consulting Limited, trading as Yappman Data, registered in England and Wales, company number 16120444. Contact: info@yappman.com.

What this is about. The Greenhouse & Lever Jobs Feed is an Apify Actor. A customer names companies; the Actor reads the job adverts those companies publish on their public Greenhouse or Lever job boards, which anyone can read without logging in, and returns them as rows: title, location, country, department, dates and a link to the employer's own advert. Optionally the advert text. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Greenhouse, Lever or any employer.

What personal details can be in an advert. Employers sometimes write a recruiter's or hiring manager's name, email address, phone number or social profile link into an advert, or name a colleague ("you will report to ..."). That is the personal data this notice is about. We do not collect applicants, applications, CVs, employee lists or anything behind a login.

What we do about it. We do not want those details, so the Actor removes the ones its rules recognise (email addresses and spelled-out forms, phone numbers, links to the social, messaging and booking sites it lists, @handles, and, in the advert text, "Recruiter: ..." lines and names that follow a contact phrase) before a row is returned, and checks each row a second time. This is best effort. It does not catch everything: a name with no contact wording around it, for example, is not removed. A person's name, or a contact detail written in a form the rules do not know, can still appear in a row.

Whose runs are whose. When a customer runs the Actor, the rows go to the customer's own Apify account. We do not receive or see them, unless the customer chooses to share a run or storage with us, and the customer decides what to read and what to do with the rows. For our own runs (daily checks, example runs and tests) the rows are held in our Apify account and we are responsible for them.

Why we handle it, and on what basis. The purpose is information about vacancies: who is advertising which jobs, and when a job has left a board's list. Employers publish the adverts so they are seen widely. Our basis is legitimate interests: ours in providing that information as a data product, and our customers' in using it for market research, recruitment analytics and job alerts. A person's contact details add nothing to that purpose, which is why the Actor removes the ones it recognises.

Who gets it. Customers who run the Actor, for their own runs. Apify, which stores the data of our own runs. Apify says in its privacy policy that it processes data primarily in the European Union and the United States, and refers to other valid transfer mechanisms. Its data processing addendum says data may be processed in any other country where Apify or its subprocessors operate, and names the EU standard contractual clauses and the UK Addendum to them.

How long we keep it. From customers' runs: nothing, we hold no copy. In our account, the results of our own runs are kept in unnamed storage, which Apify deletes after 31 days, unless we delete it sooner. Apify also keeps our ten most recent runs for 4 months, so the results of those runs can be held for that long. The saved copy our daily checks use holds titles, locations, countries, departments, links and the time each job was last seen, and is kept while the checks run. If an employer wrote a person's name into a job title, location or department, it would be in that copy too. A customer's own saved copy is in the customer's account and stays until the customer deletes it.

Your rights. You can ask us for a copy of anything we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to erase it, ask us to restrict our handling of it, and object to our handling of it. Write to info@yappman.com with the company's name and, if you can, the job. We aim to reply within 5 working days. If you have found your contact details in a row, tell us: we will tighten the rule that missed them where a rule can catch it, delete the detail from anything in our own account, and tell you what we did. We cannot recall rows a customer has already received, and we cannot change the advert itself: that is the employer's, and asking the employer to take a personal detail out of it stops it being read from the source again. We make no decisions about you by automated means and do no profiling.

Employers. If you are an employer and want your board left out, write to the same address. We may ask you to show that you speak for the employer. We will add your board to the list the Actor does not read, in its next build, withdraw older builds where the platform allows, and tell you when that is done.

Complaints. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk.
