AU Online Specials Pulse

Week-on-week counts of marked-down lines at Coles, Woolworths and Aldi Australia. Counts only: no product names, ids, prices, descriptions, images or URLs.

No week has been published yet.

What it measures

Counts of lines whose posted online shelf price is marked down against a shown was-price, week on week, at the named retailers: Coles, Woolworths and Aldi Australia. Each week has one row per retailer, and every count comes with its denominator, the lines compared.

It is not a product list and not a price comparison between retailers.

How to read it

  • Counts from weekly Wednesday samples of the posted online shelf price, one store per retailer; counts describe our sample, not a retailer’s whole range.
  • Coles is a partial staples-first slice of its range, not the whole of it; lines seen in only one of the two weeks are not compared.
  • A line is marked down when its shown was-price is above its shown price. Multibuy offers (such as “2 for $5”) and conditional offers (such as spend-and-save liquor offers or bonus credits) are counted apart, never as a markdown.
  • Aldi Australia shows a was-price on very few regular lines, so in our samples its marked-down counts are small or zero; that describes what its website shows, not what Aldi sells.
  • “Started” and “ended” are changes of state between the two weeks: a line that moves to or from a multibuy or conditional offer counts as ended or started.
  • Independent: not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any retailer. Not a statement about any retailer’s compliance with any law.

Columns

  • lines_compared: lines present in both weeks (the denominator of every count)
  • marked_down_prev / marked_down_now: compared lines marked down in each week
  • started / ended: marked down in one week, not in the other (a move to or from a multibuy or conditional offer counts)
  • deeper / shallower / steady: marked down in both weeks; the depth moved 5 percentage points or more, or less
  • multibuy_now / conditional_now: offers counted apart (not markdowns)
  • newly_ranged / left_range: lines seen in only one of the two weeks (not compared)
  • excluded_lines: pack size changed, or a suspect price move
  • median_depth_pct: median of (1 - price / was-price) over compared marked-down lines now, in percent (newly ranged lines are not included; left empty when fewer than 5 lines are marked down)

Data

The table is published as the dataset au-specials-pulse on Apify, one vintage per week. The download link is added at publish. The licence is CC BY 4.0.
