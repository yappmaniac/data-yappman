UK shelf prices: Other

How the posted online shelf prices of products in no other category moved at Tesco, Sainsbury’s, Asda and Aldi, from the free UK Online Shelf-Price Index.

Some links to Apify are affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission.

Latest published week 2026-W45: level 96.0, -0.12% on the week before (base 100 at 2026-W42). Published 9 November 2026. Shelf prices only; it is not CPI and not a measure of prices paid.

This category holds the products the keyword rules place in no other category, so it is a mixed group.

The chart shows the current series only. Its vertical axis does not start at zero, so small moves look larger.

What this does and does not show

  • The history starts on 27 September 2026. Week 41 has no data (see the notice), so the series restarted at week 42 with a new base of 100; the earlier weeks are a closed series and are not joined to the new one.
  • Categories are assigned by keyword rules on the retailers’ category names and product names, so they are approximate, and a category can rest on fewer than four retailers (method).
  • Matched products are those priced in both weeks. A retailer cell with fewer than 20 usable products keeps the week before’s level and is shown here as “not updated”. The figures are the free index’s own; nothing is added.
