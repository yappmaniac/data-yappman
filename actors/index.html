Our Actors on the Apify Store

Each tool runs on the Apify Store. Prices and terms are on each Store page.

  • UK supermarket prices: Weekly catalogue and a daily 212-item basket.
  • AU supermarket prices: Aldi Australia shelf prices by keyword, category or product page: price, unit price, was price and Special Buys drops in one row shape.
  • UK property listings: for sale and to rent from Rightmove and OnTheMarket on one schema, plus Rightmove sold prices. Zoopla is not available at the moment; OnTheMarket is opt-in.
  • Public contracts: Search UK public procurement notices. $0.005 a notice ($5.00 per 1,000) plus $0.005 a run.
  • LEI lookup: Legal Entity Identifier records by LEI, name or registration status. $0.001 an entity ($1.00 per 1,000), no start fee.
  • UK importers and exporters: HMRC trade lists by month, under the Open Government Licence. $0.005 a run plus $0.002 a trader row. Not affiliated with HMRC.

The free weekly indices are on the UK and AU index pages. How the data is collected and checked is on the methodology and data quality pages.
