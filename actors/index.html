Our Actors on the Apify Store

Each tool runs on the Apify Store. Prices and terms are on each Store page.

Some links to Apify are affiliate links: if you sign up through them we may earn a commission.

  • UK supermarket prices: Weekly catalogue and a daily 212-item basket. Free data from the same retailers: the UK Christmas dinner basket.
  • AU supermarket prices: Aldi Australia shelf prices by keyword, category or product page: price, unit price, was price and Special Buys drops in one row shape.
  • UK property listings: for sale and to rent from Rightmove and OnTheMarket on one schema, plus Rightmove sold prices. Zoopla is not available at the moment; OnTheMarket is opt-in. Free housing-cost data: the council tax Band D table.
  • Google Hotels rates: Google Hotels results and per-booking-site rates, by city or hotel. $0.003 per hotel, plus $0.005 a run start; vendor-rate and price-history rows are priced separately.
  • Public contracts: Search UK public procurement notices. $0.005 a notice ($5.00 per 1,000) plus $0.005 a run.
  • LEI lookup: Legal Entity Identifier records by LEI, name or registration status. $0.002 an entity ($2.00 per 1,000), no start fee.
  • CRICOS register lookup (Australia): Australian education providers registered to enrol overseas students (CRICOS): search by name, state, type or postcode, or look up codes. One row per provider with its type, state, postcode, city, capacity and course counts. Names are left out when they show no organisation cue. $0.002 a row ($2.00 per 1,000), no start fee. Not affiliated with the Department of Education.
  • UK importers and exporters: HMRC trade lists by month, under the Open Government Licence. $0.005 a run plus $0.002 a trader row. Not affiliated with HMRC.
  • UK sponsor register changes: Dated changes to the UK register of licensed sponsors (Worker and Temporary Worker routes): organisations added, no longer listed, and rating or route changes, one row per change. Entries that look like an individual's name are designed to be left out and counted. About half of the entries are left out (53% of change events and 54% of register entries on 11 November 2026), because the rule also holds back some two-word company names. $0.003 a change row ($3.00 per 1,000), no start fee. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Not affiliated with the Home Office or UK Visas and Immigration.
  • CQC England care locations: Search and look up care locations in the Care Quality Commission’s directory of registered locations in England, and list what changed between its files: one fixed row per location or per change, with no ratings in this version. Locations whose names could be a person’s are designed to be left out and counted, so only part of CQC’s file is shown. $0.005 a run, $0.004 a location row and $0.003 a change row. Contains CQC information, licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Not affiliated with the Care Quality Commission.
  • Aldi Netherlands and Germany prices: Aldi shelf prices in the Netherlands, Germany and five more countries.
  • Serbia grocery prices: Serbian grocery prices by barcode (EAN), product name in Latin or Cyrillic, or category, read from the price lists larger retailers publish on the national Open Data Portal.
  • Ireland rent index: Average monthly rent in Ireland by place, number of bedrooms, property type and quarter (CSO table RIQ02), and the CSO monthly residential property price index (HPM09). One fixed row per figure.
  • Australian contract notices (AusTender): Look up, search and monitor Australian Government contract awards (AusTender contract notices), read from the AusTender API.
  • Approved food establishments (FSA): Approved food establishments in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, from the Food Standards Agency’s daily file: lookup, search and changes. Scotland is not covered. Not affiliated with the Food Standards Agency.
  • GB gambling business register: Look up and watch the Gambling Commission’s register of gambling businesses for Great Britain: accounts, licences, trading names and domain names as published, with dated register changes listed 15 days or more after we first see them. Not affiliated with the Gambling Commission.
  • UK and EU product recalls: Search and monitor product recalls and safety alerts from UK OPSS, UK FSA and UK MHRA and from EU Safety Gate, in one fixed row schema. Free sample: UK and EU recalls.
  • EU airport passengers by month and port freight (Eurostat): Eurostat airport passengers, flights and freight, route pairs and port passengers and freight for EU and EFTA countries, with source and release date on every row. Free sample: EU airport months and port quarters.

The free indices are on the UK and AU index pages. How the data is collected and checked is on the methodology and data quality pages.
