Notice: matchKey changes

UK Supermarket Price Scraper on the Apify Store. Posted 5 October 2026. Ships no earlier than 4 November 2026.

Announced change to matchKey (UK Supermarket Price Scraper), 5 October 2026. Ships no earlier than 4 November 2026. Unit words in product names are written in the singular, so "2 Pints" and "2 Pint" give the same key. Pint-sized volumes are written in pints: a pack within 0.5% of a whole number of UK pints becomes <n>pt, so "2.272L", "2.27L", "2272ml" and "4 pint" all become 4pt; pint-sized means 1 to 8 pints (per unit of a multipack, so "2x568ml" becomes 2x1pt), and larger volumes such as a 70 L bin bag stay in ml; other sizes stay in ml and g. For one release, UK rows also carry matchKeyV1, the key exactly as built today, so existing joins keep working while you switch; matchKeyV1 is removed in the release after that, with its own notice. The same release also adds the fields matchBasis (which parts of the row went into its matchKey) and matchConfidence (0 to 1: the measured chance that another retailer's row with the same matchKey is the same product); both are additive. No existing field, price or event changes. UK edition only; AU rows are unaffected.
