Notice: sold-prices mode changes in version 0.3 (not before 27 November 2026)

UK Property Listings on the Apify Store. First posted 5 October 2026. Amended 27 October 2026.

From version 0.3 of UK Property Listings, mode: "sold-prices" will read sold prices from our own monthly copy of HM Land Registry's Price Paid Data instead of Rightmove's sold-price pages. Listings mode (search and listing) does not change.

What the amendment changes. Version 0.3 now changes more than the first notice said: rows carry no HM Land Registry transaction id, full postcodes are no longer accepted as input, and rows carry the postcode sector and the outcode only, with no coordinates. Because of that, the earliest release date moves from 5 November to 27 November 2026, at least 30 days after this amended notice.

What changes

  • One row per sale, not per property. Each row is one registered sale, with its price and date, plus tenure, newBuild, category and localAuthority. The propertyId, sales, lastSoldPrice, lastSoldDate, area and landRegistryUpdatedAt fields go. portal becomes "landregistry".
  • No transaction id. Rows do not carry HM Land Registry's transaction id, so rows cannot be joined across runs by that id. (New in this amendment.)
  • Postcode sector and outcode only. postcode holds the postcode sector (for example LS6 3), never the full postcode; outcode stays. latitude, longitude and coordinatesSource are always null for sold-price rows. (New in this amendment: the first notice said postcode level with the postcode's centroid.)
  • Inputs. Postcode sectors, outcodes and local authority names. Full postcodes are not accepted: they are skipped, with a note in the run summary. Rightmove /house-prices/ URLs are no longer accepted. (New in this amendment: the first notice listed full postcodes as an input.)
  • History back to 1995. A run with no dates set looks back 10 years; soldWithinYears (up to 40) or soldFrom reaches back to 1 January 1995.
  • Every registered sale in the chosen category (standard-price sales by default), with no per-area limit. The 1,000-rows-per-run ceiling and maxItems stay. HM Land Registry registers sales weeks to months after completion, so the newest months are always incomplete.
  • Standard sales by default (HM Land Registry's category A, the sales Rightmove's sold pages show). saleCategory: "additional" (repossessions, buy-to-let and company purchases, and transfers not at market value) or "any" widens it.
  • Bedrooms and bathrooms are opt-in (includeBedsBaths, off by default), filled only where a sale matches Rightmove's sold record exactly, so they are often empty.
  • Price. From version 0.3, sold-price falls from $3.00 to $2.00 per 1,000 rows (higher plans $1.80, $1.60 and $1.40 per 1,000), plus the per-run start event, only when rows are returned. Until 0.3 is released, sold-price rows stay at $3.00 per 1,000.

What does not change

  • No street address in any row.
  • England and Wales only.
  • Sale prices and dates are HM Land Registry's, and local authorities come from the ONS Postcode Directory; both are under the Open Government Licence.

What to do

If you call sold-prices mode from code or a scheduled task, check your inputs and the fields you read against the list above before 27 November 2026. In particular, replace full postcodes with postcode sectors, and stop relying on a transaction id or on coordinates in sold-price rows. The full details will be in the README and CHANGELOG with the release.
