Privacy and cookies

Yappman Data, operated by Abley-Yapp Consulting Limited.

This notice explains what personal data the Yappman Data website at data.yappman.com (including its Notices and Shelf-Price Index pages) collects about visitors, why, and your choices. The data products themselves describe retail prices and contain no personal data.

Who we are

The controller is Abley-Yapp Consulting Limited, registered in England and Wales. Contact: info@yappman.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Website analytics (only if you accept cookies). If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we load Google Analytics 4. It records pages viewed, approximate location, device, browser and referring site, and sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_<id>, which last up to two years. We use this to see which pages are used and to improve the site. Our lawful basis is your consent. If you do not accept, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.
  • Your cookie choice. We store a flag, yp-data-cookie-consent, in your browser’s local storage so the banner does not reappear. It never leaves your browser.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page. We do not have access to these logs for our own use. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • The Shelf-Price Index page. It loads the published index data directly from Apify’s servers (api.apify.com), so Apify also receives your IP address when you view that page. See Apify’s privacy policy.
  • If you email us. We keep your message and email address to reply and to keep a record of the correspondence. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests (answering enquiries and, where relevant, taking steps towards a licence).

Who receives data

Google (analytics, only if you accept), GitHub (hosting) and Apify (index data), each acting as a service provider as described above. Google may process data outside the UK; it relies on safeguards such as the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses or the UK–US data bridge. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Google Analytics keeps event-level data for 2 months, and data linked to cookies for up to 14 months after your last visit. Emails are kept for as long as needed for the enquiry and our records, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can withdraw cookie consent at any time by clearing this site’s cookies and local storage in your browser; the banner will then appear again. You can also block Google Analytics with Google’s opt-out add-on. Under UK data protection law you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent. Email info@yappman.com. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We will update this page if what we collect changes. Last updated 6 October 2026.
