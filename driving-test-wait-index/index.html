Driving test waits in Great Britain, by test centre

In October 2026, among the 317 open test centres with a median wait, the longest was 31.5 weeks at Callander (Scotland). The shortest was 0.4 weeks at Carlisle LGV (Cars)1 (North West).

DVSA releases these figures monthly, on the second Wednesday; this page is refreshed after each release. It puts the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency’s own waiting-time figures for car practical driving tests into clean tables, by test centre and by region, from January 2019, with a rank for each centre and the change in its median wait over 3, 6 and 12 months.

This page is not published by DVSA and carries no endorsement from it: the figures are DVSA’s as published, and the only arithmetic here is the rank and the changes. It is not a booking tool: it cannot be used to find or book a test.

How to read this

The median wait is DVSA’s own figure, in weeks: half of the tests were taken within that wait. The shares show how DVSA splits the tests by how long the candidate waited. A – means DVSA gives no figure for that cell: it is blank, or DVSA has withheld it (the files keep DVSA’s code in the suppressed column). Months DVSA publishes as provisional are marked and may be revised in a later release. A centre DVSA shows as closed has no rank.

Method and source

Source: DVSA, Driving test and theory test data: cars (tables DRT122F, DRT121G and DRT121A), published monthly on the second Wednesday, with the quarterly national table.

The waits are the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) figures for car practical driving tests, as DVSA publishes them each month: table DRT122F by test centre and table DRT121G by region. medianWaitWeeks is DVSA's median wait in weeks; the share columns are DVSA's percentages of tests taken within 7 weeks, between 7 and 14 weeks, between 14 and 21 weeks and after more than 21 weeks. The only arithmetic here is rank and change: rank orders the open centres that have a median wait in a month, longest wait first, and centres with the same median share a rank; change3m, change6m and change12m are the month's median wait minus the same centre's (or region's) median wait 3, 6 and 12 months earlier, in weeks to two decimal places, and are blank when either month has no figure. DVSA's shorthand ([n] no tests conducted, [r] redacted because of 10 or fewer, [x] not available, [z] not applicable, [rv] revised, [c] confidential, and N/A) is kept in suppressed and nothing is filled in. A centre DVSA shows as closed has centreStatus closed and no rank; a centre DVSA marks closed in some months and open in others keeps one key, and changes are not adjusted for this. provisional is true for months DVSA publishes as provisional (data since April 2025, at the date of this release). This page is not published by DVSA and carries no endorsement from it. It is not a booking tool: it cannot be used to find or book a test.
